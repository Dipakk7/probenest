# Shipping Policy

## Standard Delivery
Standard domestic shipping takes 3 to 5 business days. All orders over $50 qualify for free standard delivery.

## Express & Overnight Options
Express delivery arrives within 1 to 2 business days for a flat rate of $15. Overnight shipping is available for orders placed before 12:00 PM EST.

## International Shipping
We ship internationally to over 50 countries worldwide. International shipping times range from 7 to 14 business days depending on customs processing in the destination region.
