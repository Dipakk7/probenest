# Account Management Policy

## Password Reset
To reset your account password, navigate to the login portal and click 'Forgot Password'. A secure reset link will be dispatched to your registered email address. Password reset links expire after 60 minutes.

## Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is strongly encouraged for all user accounts and strictly mandatory for administrative roles. Supported MFA methods include authenticator apps (TOTP) and SMS verification.

## Account Deletion
Users may request permanent deletion of their account and associated personal data at any time via Account Settings. Account deletion is processed within 14 days.
