# Customer Support Policy

## Support Hours
Our customer support team is available Monday through Friday from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM EST. Weekend email support is monitored periodically.

## Ticketing Priority
Standard support inquiries are addressed within 24 hours. Enterprise plan customers receive priority ticket routing with a guaranteed 2-hour response window.

## Contact Channels
Customers can reach support via email at support@demorag.example.com or directly through our interactive web widget.
