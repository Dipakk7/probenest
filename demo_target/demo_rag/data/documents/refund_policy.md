# Refund Policy

## Return Window
Customers can request a full refund within 30 days of purchase for any unused hardware or active subscription service.

## Refund Process
To initiate a refund, submit a request through the customer support portal with your order number. Approved refunds are credited to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days.

## Manual Review Exceptions
Refund requests submitted after 30 days require manual review by customer service managers. Exceptions may be granted for verified product defects or billing errors.
